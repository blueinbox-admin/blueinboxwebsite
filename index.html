How we work

Clear scope. Practical decisions.

01 / Understand

Start with the actual problem.

What needs to work better, who will use it and what do you already have? We define the essentials before choosing the tools.

02 / Build

Agree on what’s included.

Set the scope and estimate, then work through design and development. Review progress along the way, not just at the end.

03 / Launch

Make it ready for real use.

Test the important flows, work through feedback and prepare the handover. Discuss any ongoing support before launch.
